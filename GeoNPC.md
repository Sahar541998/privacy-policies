# GeoNPC: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

GeoNPC is a small fan-made game made by Sahar Levy. This page explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

## Playing as a guest

If you play as a guest, nothing about you or your games leaves your device. Training games are not sent to our servers.

## Signing in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you choose it, we store:

- **An account ID** that Apple creates for this app. It does not include your real name or email.
- **Your player name**, which you choose and can change at any time.
- **A player number**, like #1, given in the order people sign up. It is not your Apple ID.
- **Your points**, used for multiplayer modes.
- **Your multiplayer games**: where you placed your pins on the game map, the round results, and who won. The server needs these to score each match fairly.

When you play a multiplayer game, the other player sees your player name, your player number, and your points. Your pins are only shown to them after each round ends.

We do not ask Apple for your email address or your real name. Your login is kept in your device’s Keychain.

## What we do not collect

- We do not collect your device location. Pins are positions on the game map, not your GPS location.
- We do not collect contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.
- We do not use analytics, ads, or tracking across other apps and websites.
- We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

## Where the data is kept

Account and multiplayer data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), our database provider, so the game can sign you in and score matches. Supabase processes that data only to run the app. We do not sell it.

## How long we keep it

We keep account and multiplayer data until you delete your account, or until you ask us to delete it.

## Deleting your data

Open Profile and tap **Delete Account**. That removes your login, your name, your points, and your match history. You can also email **le.sahar@gmail.com** and we will delete it within 30 days.

## Children

GeoNPC is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change.
